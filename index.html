Questions

Before you get started.

Can you build a website using our school’s existing branding?

Yes. We can use your school name, logo, colours, photos and content to create a consistent online identity. The required assets and scope will be agreed first.

Can parents use the portal to view fees or results?

Portal capabilities depend on the agreed package and the workflows implemented. We will confirm exactly which functions are included before development begins.

Does the price include every third-party cost?

Not necessarily. Domain registration, hosting, payment provider charges, messaging services and extra support can vary. The quote will identify what is included and any recurring costs.
